# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## Overview

**Stranger Things** is a science-fiction and mystery series set in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana. It follows a group of friends who encounter supernatural events connected to a mysterious alternate dimension called the *Upside Down*.

## Why I Like It

- The story combines mystery, adventure, and suspense.
- The characters have strong friendships.
- Each season introduces interesting challenges.
- The setting and music create a memorable atmosphere.

## Favorite Characters

1. Eleven
2. Dustin Henderson
3. Steve Harrington

> "Friends don't lie."
